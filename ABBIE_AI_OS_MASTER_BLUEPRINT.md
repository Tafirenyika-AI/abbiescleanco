# ABBIE AI OS 3.0 — Master Engineering Blueprint
Date: 2026-09-21
Status: Implementation specification; existing repository and integrations not yet audited.

## 0. Mission and non-negotiable principles
Transform Abbie's Clean Method into a unified cleaning-business operating system serving customers, administrators, cleaners and subcontractors. Initial market: Spokane Valley, Washington, subject to owner confirmation of legal entity and service boundaries. Existing stack: GitHub repository accessible to Claude Code, Vercel hosting, Neon PostgreSQL. Primary commercial goal: acquire profitable paying customers. Supported configurable categories: residential, commercial, deep cleaning, move-in/out, short-term-rental turnovers, post-construction and specialized cleaning (only advertise services actually offered and qualified to perform).

Audit the actual repository before modifying it. Preserve working functionality, assets, branding, SEO URLs and data. No mock data presented as live, no invented integration status, no unapproved spending or payments, no fabricated reviews or credentials. Deliver functioning vertical slices with migrations, tests, observability, rollback and documented acceptance criteria. All roles require server-side authorization.

Ask only for essential missing credentials, owner approval or business facts; use adapters and sandbox modes until provided.

## 2. Product surfaces and roles
Public marketing site (guest lead and booking), customer portal, cleaner portal, verified subcontractor portal, Executive HQ/admin, and shared Abbie Chat assistant. Roles: visitor, customer, cleaner, subcontractor, dispatcher, marketer, finance, manager, owner, platform support. Permission scopes must be explicit and composable, with tenant/organization and record-level authorization. Support multiple locations and service zones without assuming active operations outside the confirmed territory. Responsive mobile-first UI, accessibility, keyboard support, localization-ready copy and time-zone-aware dates.

## 3. Product modules
### 3.1 Abbie Reach: acquisition and CRM
Lead ingestion from website forms, referrals, opt-in messaging, authorized directory/licensed B2B data, property management partnerships, approved public procurement feeds, Google/Meta lead forms where accessible. Record source, timestamp, consent/lawful contact basis, location, category, deduplication fingerprint, enrichment provenance, score explanations, lifecycle, owner, activity, attribution and suppression status. Never scrape private personal information or bypass platform restrictions. Lead stages: discovered, verified, qualified, outreach-approved, contacted, engaged, quote, won/lost, retained. Use transparent configurable lead qualification, not unverifiable AI claims. Add commercial RFP tracker with deadline, requirements, evidence, bid/no-bid review and human-approved proposal drafts. Track cost per qualified lead, cost per booked customer, revenue and contribution margin by channel/cohort.

### 3.2 Marketing studio and omnichannel distribution
Brand kit with real logo, colors, fonts, approved media, tone, service claims and location facts. AI drafts calendars, ads, landing pages, SEO briefs, email campaigns, social posts, carousel concepts and video scripts. Creative asset approval, consent for customer images, review of claims, platform policy checks, scheduling via official authorized APIs where supported. Google Business Profile: authorized business information, supported posts/reviews/performance according to actual API access. Google Ads, Search Console, analytics, Meta, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and other channels via capability-aware adapters. If APIs lack a feature, show manual publish/export workflow rather than pretending to automate. Paid campaign creation/changes require owner-set budget and explicit approval. Use conversion events with consent and deduplication. Capacity-aware promotion: recommend campaigns only in areas with verified service capacity. Experiment tracking and creative versioning. Do not automatically post fabricated testimonials, before/after images or unsupported guarantees.

### 3.3 Estimates, bookings, dispatch and communications
Configurable service catalog, territories, ZIP codes, travel time, cleaner capacity, add-ons, exclusions, minimums, labor, materials, travel, overhead, target margin, taxes/fees as applicable. Pricing uses deterministic rules and administrator-controlled rate cards; AI may explain, not invent final prices. Show preliminary quote and scope assumptions; require review for unusual/specialized jobs. Booking state machine: draft, quoted, pending confirmation/payment, confirmed, assigned, en route, in progress, completed, QA pending, invoiced, paid, canceled/disputed. Prevent double booking with transaction-safe capacity reservation. Customer chooses genuine available slots, can guest checkout, request reschedule, manage recurring appointments. Dispatch optimizes qualifications, geography, time windows, travel and job margin; assignment requires eligibility and confirmation.

Notification service: event-driven transactional email/SMS, booking confirmation, reminders at configurable offsets (e.g. 24h/2h), arrival update, completion, receipt/invoice, honest review request, recurring renewal. Two-way inbound messages where provider permits, opt-in/opt-out and marketing-vs-transactional classification, quiet hours and local timezone (Spokane: America/Los_Angeles). Template versioning, delivery receipts, idempotency keys, cancellation/rescheduling of queued reminders, escalation to human, no AI call for standard templates. Comply with applicable email/SMS and consumer protection rules; legal review before broad automated outreach.

### 3.4 Abbie Vision: multimodal customer instructions
Customer can enter text, record/upload voice, photos or short video; accessible non-AI alternative. Direct-to-private-object-storage signed upload, MIME/size/duration validation, malware scanning where available, consent, expiration and deletion. Neon stores only metadata, access controls and object references. Async pipeline: transcribe audio, select limited video frames, produce structured scope (room, task, preferences, exclusions, hazards/uncertainties), flag ambiguous details, customer reviews/edits/approves. Never infer dimensions, hygiene or completion certainty solely from media. Share only minimum approved instructions with assigned cleaner/partner; audit access. Optional after-service photo QA with permission, human review and no claim that images prove sanitation.

### 3.5 Workforce, subcontractors and property managers
Cleaner onboarding, skills, availability, time tracking where lawful, assigned-job checklist, issue reporting and approved access notes. Subcontractor application, legal entity, documents, qualification/insurance review, service zones, contract terms, job offers, acceptance, completion evidence, disputes, payable calculation and settlement. Classification, licensing, insurance and tax obligations require jurisdiction-specific professional review. Property manager portal: multiple properties, turnovers, SLAs, consolidated invoices, permissions, service history and reports. Customer property memory stores preferences and approved instructions with edit/delete controls. CleanPass report: checklist, timestamps, authorized images, exceptions, follow-up, accessible customer feedback.

### 3.6 Finance
Provider-agnostic payment adapter (e.g. Stripe/Square if actually connected), invoices, deposits, partial payments, refunds, credit notes, expenses, supplier bills, receivables aging, payables, partner settlements, cash-flow, job/customer/service/territory contribution margin, accounting integration (e.g. QuickBooks). Distinguish bookings, billed revenue, collected cash and accounting profit. Payment webhooks verified, idempotent and reconciled; no raw card storage. For accounting system of record, either integrate with established accounting platform or implement properly balanced double-entry journal with immutable posted entries and audit. No automated bank-detail changes, payouts, refunds or financial write-offs without approved authority. Do not claim bank reconciliation without actual bank/accounting feeds.

### 3.7 Executive HQ and system operations
Role-tailored dashboards for funnel, pipeline, campaign spend, capacity, jobs, lateness, quality, retention, receivables, profitability, AI costs and integration health. Real data only; demo data clearly separated. Workflow execution history, failures, retries, dead-letter queue, webhook receipts, API token expiry, provider rate limits, Vercel/Neon/storage usage, audit trail, feature flags, alerts and kill switches. Owner can pause automation, change provider routing, set budgets and approve actions. Never expose secret values in UI or logs.

## 4. Abbie Chat: universal assistant + navigator
Persistent floating widget and full-screen assistant on public site, customer, cleaner, partner and admin portals. Text first; optional voice, image and video where relevant. Role-specific greeting and quick actions, contextual awareness of current route and permitted record, deep links, prefilled forms, guided workflows and handoff to human support. Guest can access public services and create inquiry, never private account data. Authenticated customers see only own records; cleaners only assigned work; partners only eligible/assigned jobs and own settlements; admins scoped by permission.

Three interaction levels: (1) navigate via typed route registry and deterministic intent matching, (2) retrieve/explain authorized data through typed server tools, (3) propose and execute authorized actions after risk-appropriate confirmation. Examples: 'take me to my invoices', 'where is my cleaner', 'show overdue invoices', 'create a Facebook draft', 'show failed integrations', 'find available partners'. A route registry has route_id, path template, allowed roles, required permissions, parameter schema and supported actions. Never generate arbitrary URLs or execute raw SQL from model output. Tool registry includes get_my_booking, search_my_invoices, get_admin_revenue_summary, get_lead_pipeline, list_assigned_jobs, list_eligible_partner_jobs, get_integration_health, create_campaign_draft, propose_reschedule, confirm_reschedule, etc. Every tool rechecks server authorization and tenant boundaries; log tool calls and redact PII. Return sources/record timestamps for operational answers, uncertainty and escalation for unavailable data. Retrieved documents and customer messages are untrusted prompt-injection surfaces. Protect against cross-role data leakage. Accessibility, keyboard controls, clear disclosure that user is talking to AI, human escalation, consent and transcript retention controls.

## 5. Architecture and provider portability
Prefer existing compatible framework after audit; target maintainable TypeScript modular monolith with isolated domain modules and background workers. Next.js/Vercel frontend where already present, Neon PostgreSQL, private object storage (S3/R2 or existing), durable job scheduler/queue compatible with Vercel execution constraints, observability and CI. Domain modules expose stable interfaces and events. Define AIProvider, EmailProvider, SMSProvider, PaymentProvider, SocialProvider, MapsProvider, StorageProvider, AccountingProvider, AuthProvider and LeadSourceProvider. Each adapter declares capabilities, auth scopes, rate limits, cost metadata, health and failure semantics. Admin integration hub supports OAuth connect/reconnect/revoke, secret references, token expiry alerts, webhook verification, sync cursors, test/sandbox mode and provider switching. Changing payment provider does not assume token/subscription portability. Never hard-code API keys or provider-specific models in business logic. Do not assume every social network permits every publishing/messaging feature.

Suggested domains/tables (adapt to existing schema): organizations, locations, service_zones, users, identities, roles, permissions, memberships, customers, properties, property_preferences, leads, lead_sources, lead_activities, opportunities, campaigns, creatives, campaign_approvals, attribution_events, services, rate_cards, quotes, quote_items, bookings, booking_events, availability, jobs, job_assignments, job_checklists, media_assets, media_consents, ai_briefs, cleaners, partners, partner_documents, partner_offers, inspections, service_reports, conversations, messages, message_templates, notification_jobs, invoices, invoice_lines, payments, payment_events, refunds, expenses, bills, partner_payables, journal_entries (only if building ledger), integrations, provider_credentials_refs, webhook_events, workflow_runs, ai_requests, ai_usage, approvals, audit_logs, feature_flags. Organization ID and appropriate foreign keys/indexes on all tenant data; data minimization and retention.

## 6. Cost and scale engineering
Do not make an AI call for navigation with known route, reminders, pricing math, date arithmetic, financial sums or lead deduplication. Cache stable FAQ/knowledge with version keys and access-safe scoping; cache AI outputs keyed by normalized input, prompt/model version, tenant and data version, with TTL and invalidation. Small model for classification, higher capability for complex proposals; OpenAI and Anthropic through adapters, no fixed assumption that one is always cheaper. Track actual token counts, image/audio/video usage, retries, latency, per-task cost, daily/monthly budget, per-user quota and alerts. Limit context, structured outputs, batch eligible nonurgent tasks, exponential backoff and bounded retries. Direct upload media; process selected frames rather than whole videos by default. Neon pooled connections, index and EXPLAIN slow queries, avoid N+1, cursor pagination, aggregate/report tables, webhook updates instead of polling, bounded cron, retention/archival and backups. Verify current Neon/Vercel/provider quotas and prices from official sources before committing budget.

## 7. Security, privacy and compliance gates
OAuth for Google/Apple/Facebook only after configuring developer apps and allowed redirect URLs; email login and guest booking; MFA for privileged roles. Secure sessions, CSRF where relevant, input validation, per-tenant/record RBAC, audit logs, encrypted sensitive data, signed uploads, media deletion, secret manager, rate limits, abuse prevention, incident response, backups and restore drills. Customer consent for recordings, photos, marketing, analytics and communications as applicable. No fabricated service claims or testimonials. Review applicable Washington business licensing, subcontractor classification, privacy, advertising, payment, tax and communications requirements with professionals. No blanket claims of legal compliance without review.

## 8. API contracts, events and state machines
Define typed OpenAPI or equivalent for domain APIs. Public endpoints rate-limited; private endpoints authenticated and authorized. All writes validate idempotency keys where replay possible. Events include lead.created, quote.accepted, booking.confirmed, booking.rescheduled, job.assigned, job.completed, invoice.issued, payment.succeeded, payment.failed, campaign.approved, integration.failed, media.processed. Outbox pattern or equivalent for reliable event delivery; inbox/webhook deduplication. Every asynchronous workflow has explicit state, retries, timeout, cancellation, DLQ and operator replay. Payment and booking state machines must reject invalid transitions. Maintain audit trail of who/what/when and previous/new state where appropriate.

## 9. Delivery plan and acceptance gates
Phase 0 audit + architecture + baseline tests + environment inventory. Phase 1 CRM and public lead-to-quote vertical slice with tracking and anti-spam. Phase 2 booking, capacity, confirmations, reminders, customer portal and auth. Phase 3 payments/invoices and financial reporting with verified webhooks. Phase 4 cleaner/partner operations, property manager portal and CleanPass. Phase 5 Abbie Chat read-only, role-aware route navigation and live queries; then confirmed action tools. Phase 6 Abbie Vision upload/processing/confirmation. Phase 7 marketing integrations, approvals, lead sourcing and attribution. Phase 8 forecasting, optimization, hardening, load tests, security review and staged rollout. Order can be adjusted for revenue impact, but no phase skips authorization, observability or tests.

Acceptance examples: a lead from website appears once in CRM with source; quote is deterministic and auditable; two users cannot reserve same capacity; rescheduling updates/removes old reminders; SMS opt-out blocks marketing; video is private and deletion works; customer cannot retrieve other customer's invoice via Abbie Chat; cleaner cannot view finance; unapproved campaign cannot publish or spend; duplicate payment webhook does not duplicate revenue; expired social token surfaces actionable alert; dashboard reports reconcile to underlying records; provider outage degrades gracefully; every AI request respects budget and logs cost; automated CI tests pass and rollback documented.
